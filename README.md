\# Women's Clothing E-Commerce Reviews: Customer Satisfaction \& Product Experience Analysis



\## About the Project



This project examines women’s reviews of e-commerce purchases. Our primary goal is to understand which features of the products customers are satisfied or dissatisfied with and to support this understanding with quantitative data. Rather than simply considering the ratings given in the reviews, we aim to gain a better understanding of customers’ experiences.



\## Business Question



Which product attributes are associated with customer satisfaction and dissatisfaction?



\## Key Findings



\- Reviews mentioning \*\*quality\*\* had an \*\*8.06 percentage-point higher\*\* dissatisfaction rate than reviews that did not mention quality.

\- Reviews mentioning \*\*fabric/material\*\* had a \*\*4.19 percentage-point higher\*\* dissatisfaction rate.

\- Reviews mentioning \*\*comfort\*\* had a \*\*7.77 percentage-point lower\*\* dissatisfaction rate, with \*\*87.52%\*\* receiving a positive rating.



\## Approach



\- Cleaned and prepared the dataset and customer review text.

\- Analyzed review vocabulary to identify common product-related terms.

\- Compared customer ratings and dissatisfaction rates across product attributes.

\- Used Chi-square tests and Cramér's V to evaluate the associations between attribute mentions and customer dissatisfaction.
